Mastery delivers: “Sovereign AI Begins with Sovereign Humans”

THE LIVE SUMMIT, Barcelona, 2026.

Last week in Barcelona, I delivered my keynote and contributed to the AI, Privacy & Leadership panel at THE LIVE SUMMIT.

It was a privilege to engage with hundreds of family office principals, investors, founders, and global leaders on what I believe to be the most consequential challenge of our time.

My message was clear:

We are not facing an AI crisis. We are facing a human consciousness crisis in the age of AI.

Technology can only be as sovereign as the human beings building, governing, and wielding it.

AI amplifies the consciousness of those who use it. And as technological sophistication accelerates, our capacity for discernment, independent judgment, and conscious decision-making must advance alongside it.

The greatest risk is not simply the speed of technological advancement, but the underdevelopment of human sovereignty in those entrusted with its power.

At MASTERY. we are building the leadership infrastructure and technology to bridge this widening gap, advancing human sovereignty alongside the accelerating sophistication of AI.

Because the future of AI leadership demands more than technological intelligence. It demands mastery of the human intelligence directing it.

Sovereign AI demands sovereign human beings.

And that is a matter of MASTERY.
